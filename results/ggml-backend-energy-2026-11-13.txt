# ggml backend GEN9: energy per token (RAPL), FFN fusion on/off vs ggml-cpu, Qwen2.5-1.5B F16, pp512, 2026-11-13
# Method: bench/compare/rapl_run.sh around a whole llama-bench process (-p 512 -n 0); each config run with -r 2 and
# -r 10, energy per token = (E(r10) - E(r2)) / (8 * 512): load, warm-up and first-use packing cancel out. RAPL domains:
# package (whole SoC), core (CPU cores), uncore (on this client part mostly the GPU), dram. 30 s idle before each
# process so every run starts from a similar temperature; configs alternate; 3 rounds.
# Why: fusion was 2-5 % slower (CPU clock 2500 -> 1750 MHz under the shared 25 W limit). Open question was whether it
# still saves energy (fewer GPU kernels: pk_a3 279 -> 118 ms, epilog 200 -> 41 ms per run).

## Prediction (written before measuring)
[HYP] Package power is at the limit in both GEN9 configs (22-27 W average over the 8 extra reps), so energy per token
      follows 1/throughput: fused 0.14-0.16 J/token, unfused 0.13-0.15 J/token, fused NOT lower than unfused
      (difference 0 to +8 %). The uncore share is higher in unfused (more GPU kernel time) but the core share is
      higher in fused only if the CPU is not clock-limited... expected: core lower in fused, uncore lower in fused, but
      the total is what matters and it is set by PL1 x time.
[HYP] ggml-cpu (no GEN9): 0.4-0.6 J/token, i.e. GEN9 is 3-4x more energy-efficient per prompt token.
Falsified if: fused is >= 3 % lower in J/token than unfused (fusion saves energy), or GEN9/CPU ratio < 2.5x or > 5x.

## Against the prediction
- "Fused NOT lower than unfused (0 to +8 %)": FALSIFIED. Fusion is 10.7 % LOWER in energy per token. The mistake: I
  assumed the package sits at PL1 in both cases so energy ~ time; it does not (20-22.5 W average, below 25 W), and the
  lower CPU clock under fusion makes the CPU's share cheaper per operation.
- GEN9 vs ggml-cpu 3-4x: confirmed in measurement 1 (3.2x; ggml-cpu not repeated here).
Consequence for the default: fusion trades 1-3 % speed for ~11 % energy. Kept opt-in (GEN9_FFN=1) for now; on battery
it is the better setting.
