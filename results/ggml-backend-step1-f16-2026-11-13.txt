# ggml backend GEN9, step 1 F16 weights (design D0), 2026-11-13 -- docs/ggml-backend-design.md sections 4-6
# Path: F16 weights packed once for the accurate FP16 GEMM (GEN9BLAS_HS, pk_b16_t); F32 activations packed straight to
# FP16 (new kernel pk_a3_f32, round to nearest); main kernel hgemm_kc_v (32 FP16 products, then FP32 sums) writes FP16 into
# a work buffer; new epilogue gen9blas_epilog_hf converts to F32 and raises a flag on non-finite values; a flagged call is
# recomputed with the weights converted to F32 (SGEMM). No change to the assembly kernels.
# Deviation from the design: the result is rounded to FP16 once (the asm kernel stores FP16), ~2^-11 relative.
