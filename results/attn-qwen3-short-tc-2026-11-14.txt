## Reading
- Fixed cost per node ~0.6 ms (n 32 and 64 take the same time): pinning + submit + wait. Below n ~ 150 the CPU is
  faster; the library's n_q < 32 cut-off is too low for short texts [MEAS].
- Typical document chunk (~430 tokens, causal): ~200-225 GFLOPS, 2.0-2.2x the CPU; long prompts reach 290-300.
- In the batched embedding run on gp (-ub 512): 5740 launches for 3556 nodes; at ~0.6 ms fixed cost that is ~3 s of
  the 16.9 s attention time [EST]. One launch per node for all runs (plan step 2) removes the repeated fixed cost and
  fills the GPU better; larger -ub (more texts per node) works in the same direction without kernel changes -> the gp
  -ub 512/1024/2048 sweep decides whether step 2 is worth its cost.
