Reading
- One step's weight GEMMs cost ~21-25 ms (skinny, M <= 8, ~14 GB/s) or ~33 ms (packed, M <= 16, ~9.5 GB/s) almost
  independently of M: the weights are read once per step, the rows are nearly free. Per token: M = 1 21 ms (CPU Q8_0
  13.4 ms), M = 8 3.0 ms, M = 16 2.1 ms, M = 32 1.16 ms, M = 64 0.70 ms (packed, 7 GB/s). Single stream (M = 1-5) stays
  slower than or equal to the CPU, as the decoder report says; batching tokens of several segments is what pays.
- Not included, per audio segment and step: cross-attention K/V 30.7 MB (shared by the beams of one segment, not
  across segments) ~2.4 ms at 13 GB/s; self-attention and the elementwise ops; one wait per step instead of 33 (the
  33 waits here are in the numbers, ~1-2 ms).
- Estimate per decoded token incl. cross-attention (weights at the measured ms/step + N x 2.4 ms):
    greedy, N segments (M = N):  N = 8 ~5.4 ms, N = 16 ~4.5 ms, N = 32 ~3.6 ms        (CPU Q8_0 13.4 ms)
    beam 5, N segments (M = 5N): N = 8 (M = 40) ~61 ms per step = 7.6 ms per segment-step (CPU 25 ms) -> ~3.3x
- With Q8_0 read directly (no FP16 cache) the weight part would halve; kp (dual channel) not measured yet (busy).
